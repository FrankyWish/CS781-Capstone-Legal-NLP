## Reading this table

Rows 1-8 are the four architectures, each raw and then with the staged decoding sweep. Rows 9 onward are system-level: MBR over pooled candidates, then corpus retrieval spliced over the MBR output.

**Row 16 is the graded submission.** It is a hybrid: 308 of 1000 continuations are exact text retrieved from the permitted pretraining corpus, 692 are generated by the models. The best **generation-only** result is row 9 at **0.8449**, and that is the number that measures the model. See EXPERIMENTS.md SS11.4.

Scores marked 'submitted, not top-11' were uploaded but the leaderboard only shows each participant's promoted best, so their exact values were not recorded. Their dev scores are in EXPERIMENTS.md SS10.
